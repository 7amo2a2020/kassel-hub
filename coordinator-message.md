# Message to the group coordinator / admin

Send this (English) to the WhatsApp International Students group admin/coordinator **before** posting anything.
It's framed clearly as a **volunteer effort** — no cost, no personal gain.

---

Hi! I'm Mohamed, a new Master's student joining the University of Kassel this winter semester (EECS).

I'd like to do a small **volunteer project** for the community — completely free, nothing commercial, and nothing in it for me personally. I just want to make it easier for new students to settle in.

I've noticed a lot of people in the group keep asking the same things — *"anyone from [country]?"*, *"anyone in [program]?"*, *"anyone in shared room X?"* — and the messages get buried really fast, so people can't find each other.

So, on my own time, I built a **simple, free web page** to help, and I'd love your permission before sharing it here:

• **Find your groups** — a student picks their country, study program, and dorm, and instantly gets the right WhatsApp group links to join.
• **Be findable** — an optional form so people can list themselves and others can find them.
• **Get to Kassel** — a short travel guide (which airport, the Frankfurt → ICE → Kassel-Wilhelmshöhe route) to help newcomers arrive.

It's **completely opt-in**, collects only basic info people choose to share, uses **join-by-link** groups (nobody is added without consent, no phone numbers collected), and it's clearly marked as **student-made, not official**. I'm happy to follow whatever rules you prefer, add co-admins, and even **hand the whole thing over to the group** (or next year's students) whenever you like. I can **show you the page first** before anything goes public.

I'm not looking for anything in return — just glad to help. Would it be okay to set this up for the group? Thanks a lot! 🙌

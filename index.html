✈️ The easy route to Kassel

✈️ Frankfurt (FRA)→ 🚄 ICE ~1h35→ 🚉 Kassel-Wilhelmshöhe

Frankfurt is the best gateway: most direct international flights, and a fast direct ICE train from inside the airport to Kassel.

    🛬 Which airport to fly into

    Kassel's own airport (Kassel-Calden, KSF) has almost no scheduled flights — don't rely on it.

    🎫 Train tickets & tips

      🌍 Coming from your country?

      Same idea from anywhere: fly to Frankfurt (or a nearby hub) → ICE to Kassel-Wilhelmshöhe → tram 1/3 to your place.

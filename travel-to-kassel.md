# How to get to Kassel ✈️🚄

**Kassel's long-distance station is `Kassel-Wilhelmshöhe`** (ICE) — *not* Kassel Hbf for fast trains.
From Kassel-Wilhelmshöhe, **tram lines 1 & 3** take you into the city and toward the campus/dorms.

## The easy route (recommended for most)
**Fly to Frankfurt (FRA) → direct ICE (~1h35) → Kassel-Wilhelmshöhe.**

1. Land at **Frankfurt Airport (FRA)**, clear passport control, collect baggage.
2. Follow signs to **Fernbahnhof / Long-distance trains** (Terminal 1 — inside the airport).
3. Buy an ICE ticket to **Kassel-Wilhelmshöhe** (DB Navigator app, bahn.de, or a machine).
4. Board the ICE — about **1h35**, often direct.
5. Arrive **Kassel-Wilhelmshöhe** → **tram 1 or 3** to your accommodation.

## Which airport to fly into
| Airport | Notes | To Kassel |
|---|---|---|
| **Frankfurt (FRA)** | Best — most direct flights, ICE from inside the airport | **~1h35** ICE |
| **Hannover (HAJ)** | Good alternative | ~2h train |
| **Düsseldorf (DUS)** | More flights, longer ride | ~2.5–3h train |
| **Cologne/Bonn (CGN)** | Western hub | ~2.5–3h train |
| **Berlin (BER) / Munich (MUC)** | Only if the fare is much better | ~3.5–4.5h ICE |

⚠️ **Kassel-Calden (KSF)** — Kassel's own airport — has almost no scheduled flights. Don't rely on it.

## Tickets & tips
- Book early on **bahn.de** or the **DB Navigator** app (English) — a *Sparpreis* can be ~€18–30 vs ~€60 same-day.
- Make sure the ticket starts at **Frankfurt Flughafen Fernbahnhof** (the airport), not Frankfurt Hbf.
- Cheaper & slower: **FlixBus** to Kassel (~€10–20).
- A **seat reservation** is optional but helpful with luggage on a busy ICE.

## From Egypt (example)
🇪🇬 Fly **Cairo (CAI) → Frankfurt (FRA)** direct (~4.5h, EgyptAir / Lufthansa), then the ICE above.
Same idea from anywhere: fly to Frankfurt (or a nearby hub) → ICE to Kassel-Wilhelmshöhe → tram 1/3.

## Keep in your carry-on
Passport + visa · admission letter + enrolment certificate · blocked-account/finance & health-insurance proof · some euros + a card that works abroad · your accommodation address (offline copy).

*Durations are typical, not guaranteed — always check the live connection in DB Navigator.*
